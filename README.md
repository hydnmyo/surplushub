# Surplus Connect

Build a polished, realistic, responsive web application prototype for a startup called [SurplusHub].

The platform is an AI-powered circular B2B marketplace for surplus, reusable, and recyclable industrial materials.

CORE CONCEPT

“One business’s surplus is another business’s resource.”

The platform connects businesses that have unused, excess, leftover, reusable, or recyclable materials with other businesses that need those materials as inputs for production, packaging, manufacturing, construction, crafts, or other legitimate business activities.

The platform is NOT a normal consumer marketplace and NOT a recycling collection company.

It is a digital B2B marketplace and transaction-enabling platform that helps businesses:

Sell surplus materials

Discover affordable production resources

Find new suppliers

Find businesses looking for their surplus

Connect and negotiate

Track and verify transactions

Reuse materials instead of unnecessarily discarding them

The initial market is Myanmar, with a focus on businesses in Yangon, Mandalay, Bago, and other major commercial areas.
